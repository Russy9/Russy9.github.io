Hospital Locator System

Welcome to the Hospital Locator System.

The Hospital Locator System is a web-based application designed to help users quickly find hospitals and healthcare facilities in their area. The system provides information about recommended hospitals and allows users to access directions to their chosen healthcare facility.

Our goal is to improve access to healthcare services by making it easier for people to locate hospitals during emergencies and routine medical visits. The system also includes location services and an AI assistant to help users find relevant healthcare information.

We are committed to providing a simple, reliable, and user-friendly platform that helps connect people with the healthcare services they need.
